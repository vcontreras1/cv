## Q1. Where does a Spanish-speaking business graduate with English C1 get the best salary-to-cost ratio in innovation roles?

### Takeaway
On raw ratio, Zurich, Berlin, Paris and Munich come out on top. Stockholm also scores very high but the data is low confidence. Madrid sits around 1.6× rent. Dublin, Barcelona, London and Amsterdam come out worst, because their rents are high compared with junior pay.

Once language is factored in, **Berlin** looks like the best realistic option for an English-plus-Spanish speaker. **Lisbon** also beats Madrid in its first year thanks to IRS Jovem. Amsterdam pays well, but Valeria would not get the 30% ruling, so rent eats much of the gain.

**Special tax regimes for newcomers:**
- **Netherlands 30% ruling (Expat Scheme), 2026 thresholds.** The general salary threshold is €48,013. The lower €36,497 threshold applies only to people under 30 who hold a Dutch academic **master's** degree or a foreign equivalent. The applicant must also have been recruited from abroad and have lived more than 150 km from the Dutch border for more than 16 of the previous 24 months. The ruling lasts up to 5 years, and the employer is not obliged to pay the allowance. — [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/en/individuals/content/coming-to-work-in-the-netherlands-30-percent-facility)
- **30% ruling becomes 27% from 1 Jan 2027**, and the minimum salary standards will also change. — [business.gov.nl](https://business.gov.nl/staff/employing-staff/the-expat-scheme-30-percent-ruling-in-the-netherlands/); also [Portsight Tax](https://www.portsighttax.com/en/insights/30-procent-regeling-2026)
- **Portugal IFICI ("NHR 2.0")** gives a 20% flat tax. It requires at least an EQF level 6 degree (bachelor's) **plus 3 years of relevant professional experience**, or a PhD. It is limited to listed professions: researchers, doctors, engineers and ICT staff, and senior executives and directors (ISCO 112/12). There are also routes through certified startups and through export or well-funded companies. — [imin-portugal (2026)](https://imin-portugal.com/blog/portugal-ifici-tax-regime/); [FRESH Legal](https://fresh-legal.com/ifici-nhr-2-portugal-tax-guide/) (says certified startups under Law 21/2023 have "no degree requirement"); [EY Tax News 2025](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2025-0533-portugal-issues-regulations-on-tax-incentive-for-scientific-research-and-innovation)
- **Portugal IRS Jovem (2026).** People aged 35 or under who are not dependants get 100% of employment income exempt in year 1, 75% in years 2–4, 50% in years 5–7 and 25% in years 8–10. The year-1 cap is €29,542.15. "The count starts in the first year you have employment or self-employment income as a non-dependant, including years before 2025." — [Employsome](https://employsome.com/hire/portugal/irs-jovem/); foreign nationals who are Portuguese tax residents are eligible — [Tytle](https://www.tytle.io/en/pt/blog/irs-jovem-tax-exemption-guide); cap based on IAS €537.13 in 2026 — [AnchorLess](https://anchorless.io/blog/finance/youth-irs-benefit-portugal)

## Q2. Which cities realistically hire people without the local language for business (non-engineering) roles?

### Takeaway
English-only business hiring is realistic in **Dublin, Amsterdam, London** (visa barrier), **Berlin** (mainly startups and scale-ups), **Lisbon** (international hubs) and **Brussels** (EU-affairs bubble). It is partial in Stockholm, Copenhagen and Zurich. It is weak in Paris, Milan and Munich for marketing and brand roles that face the local consumer.

## Q4. Work-permit formalities: Switzerland (L/B) and the UK (visas; HPI and Comillas)

### Takeaway
**Switzerland:** under free movement, a Spanish citizen with a job contract simply gets an L permit (contract of 3–12 months) or a B permit (contract of 12 months or more, or open-ended; valid 5 years).

**UK:** a visa is required. **Comillas does not qualify for the HPI** (no Spanish university is on the 2025 list), the Graduate visa needs a UK degree, and the Youth Mobility Scheme excludes EU countries. A UK-EU "Youth Experience Scheme" is under negotiation but was not in force as of March 2026. That leaves only the **Skilled Worker** route: a licensed sponsor, a graduate-level role, and ≥£41,700 or the going rate. A reduced floor of £33,400 can apply in some cases. These requirements are hard to meet for a junior marketing role.

### Cited Findings
- **Swiss L EU/EFTA permit:** EU/EFTA nationals are entitled to it "provided they are in possession of an employment contract valid from three up to twelve months"; contracts under 3 months only need online notification. L permits without gainful employment are also granted to EU job seekers. — [SEM](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/en/home/themen/aufenthalt/eu_efta/ausweis_l_eu_efta.html)
- **Swiss B EU/EFTA permit:** issued to EU/EFTA citizens "holding a contract of employment of at least twelve months or of unlimited duration"; valid for five years and renewable. — [SEM](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/en/home/themen/aufenthalt/eu_efta/ausweis_b_eu_efta.html)
- **UK HPI:** you must hold a qualification from an eligible university on the list for the year of award. The 1 Nov 2025 – 31 Oct 2026 list applies to Valeria's June 2026 degree. UK universities are not eligible. — [GOV.UK HPI eligibility](https://www.gov.uk/high-potential-individual-visa/eligibility)
- **The HPI 2025 list (qualifications awarded 1 Nov 2025 – 31 Oct 2026) contains no Spanish university.** European entries include Delft, EPFL, ETH Zurich, Heidelberg, Institut Polytechnique de Paris, Karolinska, KTH, KU Leuven, Lund, PSL, Sorbonne and TUM. - **UK Skilled Worker:** needs an approved sponsor and a salary of at least the higher of £41,700 or the occupation's going rate. Some applicants can qualify at a minimum of £33,400 under the reduced-salary rules. Jobs below graduate level are eligible only if they are on the immigration salary list or temporary shortage list. — [GOV.UK Skilled Worker: your job](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/your-job)
- **Youth Mobility Scheme:** EU member states (Spain included) are not eligible in 2026 — [Rowan guide](https://withrowan.co.uk/guides/youth-mobility-eligible-countries). A UK-EU Youth Experience Scheme for 18–30-year-olds was still "envisaged" and under discussion at the 16–17 March 2026 EU-UK Parliamentary Partnership Assembly; negotiations are expected to lead to formal agreements — [EP Think Tank, Mar 2026](https://epthinktank.eu/2026/03/14/eu-uk-relations-mobility-and-youth-opportunities/); [EPRS PDF](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/RegData/etudes/ATAG/2026/782680/EPRS_ATA(2026)782680_EN.pdf)

### Inferences
- **London is only realistic in two cases:** (a) a sponsoring employer offers a graduate-level role at £33,400 or more under the reduced-salary rules (whether she qualifies under those rules must be checked on GOV.UK's "when you can be paid less" page), or (b) the YES scheme comes into force. Junior marketing pay (from £33K) sits at or below these thresholds.
- **Switzerland is administratively easy.** The real barriers are the job market (German is often needed) and the high fixed costs: mandatory health insurance and rents of about €1,900–2,600 for a 1-bed.
