## 1. FMCG/consumer rotational programmes

### Takeaway
Most big FMCG graduate schemes in Europe are country-based and usually require the local language. L'Oréal SeedZ (Germany April 2027 tracks open now) and Unilever UFLP (2027 cycle open, rolling) are the most actionable right now. Heineken's Netherlands window already closed (Sept 2026) and needed Dutch. Novo Nordisk (pharma, but an international, English-language and purpose-driven programme) opens 16 Dec 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **L'Oréal SeedZ Management Trainee Programme** (new global name for the Management Trainee programme). It is a permanent full-time role from day one, with at least 3 strategic rotations within one market. It runs in 40+ countries with ~1,200 trainees worldwide in 2026. Pay is "aligned with local market standards" (no figure published). The rule is a maximum of 3 applications per rolling 30 days, so choose carefully. — [L'Oréal SeedZ](https://careers.loreal.com/en/loreal-seedz-management-trainee-programme)
  - Open listings seen on 26 Oct 2026 **[VERIFIED]**: SeedZ Düsseldorf **April 2027** tracks in General Management, Finance and Supply Chain; Management Trainee in Zagreb; Marketing MT (Dermatological Beauty) in Istanbul. No deadline is shown on the overview page. — [L'Oréal SeedZ](https://careers.loreal.com/en/loreal-seedz-management-trainee-programme)
  - German postings are titled "(m/w/d)" in German, so German is very likely required **[ESTIMATED; check the job description]**.
  - UK: L'Oréal Management Trainee Programme London, 18 months, three 6-month rotations, £30K–£35K. A "January 2027" cycle was posted as open **[AGGREGATOR: Instagram]**. — [Instagram post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeYupdmDFE2/). UK graduate-scheme applications open "from October to March" **[AGGREGATOR]**. — [Higherin](https://higherin.com/jobs/42912/loreal/register-your-interest-graduate-schemes-2027). She would need a UK visa.
  - Nordic MT programme: two 9-month rotations, applicants must have the right to work in the country, salary listed as 35,000–40,000 (currency not given; probably SEK/month) **[AGGREGATOR]**. — [Graduateships](https://graduateships.com/companies/loreal/)
  - Alternative: **L'Oréal VIE** (Business France), a single 12–24-month assignment abroad, open to EU nationals. - **Unilever Future Leaders Programme (UFLP)**: country-run. European pages exist for the Netherlands, Germany (German-language page) and UK & Ireland. — [Unilever Early Careers](https://careers.unilever.com/en/early-careers)

### Inferences
- Best fits for an English-only, Spanish-speaking EU graduate: Novo Nordisk (English corporate language, Denmark); L'Oréal VIE; L'Oréal SeedZ in English-friendly markets (Netherlands, Nordics; confirm per posting); Unilever UFLP Netherlands (Unilever's corporate language is English, but confirm whether Dutch is required on the posting).

## 5. EU institutions and public innovation

### Takeaway
Registration for the European Commission's Blue Book October 2027 session runs **15 Feb – 12 Mar 2027**; the March 2027 session is closed. The EIB recruits graduates on a rolling, posting-by-posting basis. The OECD Young Associate 2027–29 round is postponed. Enterprise Ireland's international graduate programme closes **27 Oct 2026 at midday**.

### Cited Findings
- **EC Blue Book traineeship**: 5-month paid traineeship open to EU citizens with at least a Bachelor's degree. Registration for the October 2027 session opens 15 February 2027 at 10:00 and closes 12 March 2027 at 10:00 (Brussels time) **[VERIFIED]**. Registration for the March 2027 session is closed (deadline was 4 Sept 2026). — [Blue Book calendar](https://traineeships.ec.europa.eu/about/calendar-sessions_en); [EC news](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/become-european-commission-trainee-applications-are-now-open-2026-07-22_en)
- **EIB Graduate Programme**: lasts 1–2 years, extendable to 4. Candidates apply through the job portal to individual postings; aggregators describe rolling reviews. EIB traineeships have two intakes (Mar–Apr and Sep–Oct), announced about 4 months before each. — [EIB Graduate](https://www.eib.org/en/about/careers/categories/graduate-programme); [EIB Traineeships](https://www.eib.org/en/about/careers/categories/traineeships/index)
- **OECD Young Associate Programme**: the 2027–29 application period is postponed. The previous round (2026–28) closed 14 Dec 2025 and required undergraduate completion between 1 Jan 2025 and 1 Sep 2026, which she would have met. — [Impactpool](https://www.impactpool.org/articles/oecd-young-associates-programme-2026-2028-now-open); [OECD official page](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/careers/young-associates.html)
- **Enterprise Ireland International Graduate Programme 2027–2029**: applications open 1–27 October 2026 (midday) **[VERIFIED snippet]**. It places graduates in overseas offices supporting Irish exporters. Eligibility was not checked. — [Enterprise Ireland](https://www.enterprise-ireland.com/en/careers-at-enterprise-ireland/international-graduate-programme)
