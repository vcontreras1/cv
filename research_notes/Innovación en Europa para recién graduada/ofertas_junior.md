# Junior full-time roles (0–2 yrs) in innovation, strategy, growth/marketing and business in Europe, for Valeria (Spanish-native BBA, graduated June 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-26. Posting dates come from the employers' Greenhouse ATS data (pulled live through Firecrawl on 2026-10-26) or from search snippets. "Live" means the posting was listed on the employer's ATS on 2026-10-26. Aggregator listings (Glassdoor, ACCA, Built In, iAgora) are marked as such.

**Main finding:** Most of the "Spanish-market" roles at the big Amsterdam, Dublin and Berlin tech hubs that are open now ask for 2–5 years of experience (sales or account management). True 0–2-year roles are rarer. They sit in junior sales/AE roles, junior customer success, consulting analyst programmes, and graduate fast-track programmes.

---

## 1. Which junior role types can a BBA graduate get into, and who is hiring them now?

### Takeaway
Of the role types live in Oct 2026, the most accessible are consulting Business Analyst / Junior Consultant (strategy and pricing firms), junior commercial roles that need Spanish (Junior Account Executive, Junior Account Manager, Junior CSM), and company graduate fast-tracks (Glovo/Delivery Hero). Innovation-boutique junior roles (Board of Innovation etc.) do exist, but I found no confirmed live 2026 posting for them.

"Junior Innovation Consultant" titles exist (Board of Innovation, PNO) but are scarce and irregular.
- Many listings titled "Analyst" or "Junior" in consulting are actually internships (Roland Berger Lisbon, Kearney Madrid). Read each posting carefully.

### Gaps
- I found no live, dated Oct 2026 junior postings for Accenture Song, frog, Fahrenheit 212, Strategyzer, Innosight or Board of Innovation in Europe. Their career pages were not scraped because of the tool-call budget.
- I found no confirmed full-time junior postings for Product Marketing, Growth Marketing, Venture/CVC Analyst or Partnerships Associate with dates in Sept–Oct 2026. These need direct LinkedIn and Welcome to the Jungle searches.
- I did not verify a 2027 Glovo Early Careers Booster cohort.

## 3. What remote-from-Spain options exist?

### Takeaway
Remote-from-Spain junior roles are rare. The one confirmed remote-eligible Spanish role (Stripe Velocity AE) needs 2+ years of experience. Most hubs (Adyen, GetYourGuide, Delivery Hero) are hybrid or office-first.

### Cited Findings
- Stripe's Velocity AE role (Spanish or Italian) can be done from a "remote location", defined as 35+ miles / 56+ km from a Stripe office. Office-assigned staff spend at least 50% of their time in the office. Requires 2+ yrs sales. — [Stripe posting](https://stripe.com/jobs/search?gh_jid=8241345)
- Adyen: "office-first… we do not offer remote-only roles." — [Adyen posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/adyen/jobs/8156029)
- GetYourGuide: hybrid with 3 days in office plus 30 days a year working from anywhere. — [GYG posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/getyourguide/jobs/8225766)
- Delivery Hero tech grads in Berlin: hybrid, 2 days a week on campus (per search summary). — [Delivery Hero careers](https://careers.deliveryhero.com/job/associate-data-scientist-2026-tech-grad-program-in-berlin-germany-jid-8142)

### Inferences
- A realistic way to stay in Spain is a Madrid or Barcelona office of a European scale-up (Adyen, N26, Stripe, Glovo, Qonto Barcelona) rather than fully remote work.

### Gaps
- I did not search remote-first boards (e.g., Remote OK, Welcome to the Jungle's remote filter) for junior EU-remote roles.

## 4. Which job boards and search tactics work best, and when do junior roles get hired?

### Takeaway
Searching employer ATS boards directly (Greenhouse keyword "Spanish"/"Junior") gave the most reliable, dated data. Strategy consultancies recruit graduates about a year ahead, with deadlines in early autumn.

### Cited Findings
- Greenhouse boards can be searched by keyword. For example, `job-boards.greenhouse.io/<company>?keyword=Spanish` returned live, dated roles for Adyen, Stripe, N26 and GetYourGuide. — [Adyen board example](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/adyen?keyword=Spanish)
- **Consulting calendar:** Simon-Kucher Oslo set the deadline for its "Graduate position 2027" at 25.09.2026, with rolling review. — [NHH](https://www.nu.nhhs.no/jobs/2045/details). Kearney's "Business Analyst 2026" posting was published 20 July 2026. — [ZipRecruiter FR](https://www.ziprecruiter.fr/jobs/558807103-kearney-business-analyst-2026-a-kearney). Roland Berger Lisbon closes 21 Nov 2026. — [ACCA](https://jobs.accaglobal.com/job/14224215/junior-business-analyst/)
- **Corporate graduate programmes:** Glovo's Booster asked for a May–Sept start. — [Delivery Hero](https://careers.deliveryhero.com/job/early-careers-booster-in-barcelona-spain-jid-8623)
- Consulting-specific aggregators that surfaced relevant postings: myconsultingoffer, Management Consulted, mbbprep, Bright Network, JobTeaser, iAgora. — [myconsultingoffer](https://jobs.myconsultingoffer.org/jobs/163128113-associate-consultant-graduate-hire); [JobTeaser example](https://www.jobteaser.com/en/job-offers/047811be-c5bc-45d0-92ef-e1689ae914f8-reply-junior-business-analyst-m-f-x-11207)

### Inferences (not separately sourced; general practice)
- LinkedIn: filter by Experience level "Entry level" and Job type "Full-time", search keywords such as "Spanish" or "Iberia", and set Date posted to "Past week".
- Welcome to the Jungle (which absorbed Otta), Sifted jobs and EURES are standard boards for EU startup and cross-border roles. I did not check them in this session.
- Hiring calendar: strategy firms run autumn campaigns (Sept–Nov) for starts the following year, with rolling hiring for off-cycle starts. Scale-ups hire juniors year-round, but cohort programmes open in spring for summer or autumn starts.

### Gaps
- I did not check Welcome to the Jungle, Sifted, EURES or LinkedIn result counts directly.

## 5. What do junior full-time roles pay, by city?

### Takeaway
None of the live postings reviewed listed a salary. The only figures available are third-party data of mixed quality: roughly €30–35k gross in Madrid (estimate), and around €31–33k for junior marketing roles in Amsterdam and Dublin. Figures for Berlin conflict.

### Cited Findings
- **Spain (national):** early-career Marketing Analyst average €33,840, range €27k–36k (Payscale). — [Payscale ES](https://www.payscale.com/research/ES/Job=Marketing_Analyst/Salary/62cf9974/Early-Career)
- **Spain (marketing):** junior or associate roles pay €1,600–2,300 gross per month. Madrid and Barcelona pay about 10–15% more than the rest of Spain. — [JobRise](https://jobrise.io/en/blog/marketing-salary-and-job-market-in-spain-2026/)
- **Madrid (marketing analytics):** 0–2 years about €33k (Marketing Monk). The source is internally inconsistent, so use it with caution. — [Marketing Monk Madrid](https://www.marketingmonk.so/salaries/es/madrid/marketing-analytics)
- **Amsterdam/NL:** junior Marketing Analyst about €33k gross excluding the 8% holiday allowance; entry-level digital marketing about €32k. — [Searchlab](https://searchlab.nl/en/statistics/digital-marketing-salaries-statistics-2026). Entry-level Marketing Specialist median about €31k. — [Payscale NL](https://www.payscale.com/research/NL/Job=Marketing_Specialist/Salary/06a2cdc6/Entry-Level-Amsterdam)
- **Dublin:** early-career Marketing Analyst about €31.1k (Payscale; the page has data errors). — [Payscale IE](https://www.payscale.com/research/IE/Job=Marketing_Analyst/Salary/c370a2be/Early-Career-Dublin)
- **Berlin:** early-career Marketing Analyst with Excel about €45k (Payscale, thin data). Marketing Monk shows €88k entry level, which is implausible and looks templated. — [Payscale DE](https://www.payscale.com/research/DE/Job=Marketing_Analyst/Salary/02b6c4a7/Early-Career-Berlin-Microsoft-Excel); [Marketing Monk Berlin](https://www.marketingmonk.so/salaries/de/berlin/marketing-analytics)

### Inferences
- **ESTIMATE:** junior marketing or business roles pay about €28–35k gross in Madrid and Barcelona, and about €31–40k in Amsterdam, Dublin and Berlin. Consulting analyst and Big Tech graduate roles typically pay above these bands, but I found no sourced figures for them this session.
- Adyen asks for a salary expectation on its application form, so Valeria should prepare a city-specific number.

### Gaps
- No posting reviewed listed pay. I found no reliable 2026 data for junior consulting analysts at Roland Berger, Simon-Kucher or Kearney in Madrid or Lisbon, or for Paris, Lisbon or Brussels in general.
