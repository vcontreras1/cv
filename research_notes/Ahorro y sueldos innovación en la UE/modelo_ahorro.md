# Monthly savings model for a junior (0–2 yrs, full-time) business professional: 15 EU cities + Zurich vs Madrid

Profile: Valeria. BBA from Comillas ICADE (June 2026), EU national, native Spanish speaker, English C1. Looking for full-time junior roles in marketing, strategy or innovation (no internships). Assumed age 22–23. This matters because several transport discounts stop at 23, 25 or 26.

Data accessed 26 Oct 2026. These notes build on `../Innovación en Europa para recién graduada/ciudades_europa.md`. Its Glassdoor salaries and Numbeo 1-bed rents are reused here, refreshed where new data was found.

Labels:
- **[calc]** = output of a third-party net-pay calculator.
- **[est]** = the researcher's own calculation from cited inputs. Check it with the official simulator before deciding.
- "Net/mo" always means **annual net ÷ 12**, so the 13th/14th payments in ES, PT, IT, AT and BE are spread over the year.

## Q1. Junior gross annual salary ranges for business roles, by city

### Takeaway
Typical junior business pay falls into three groups:
- **€22–30k:** Iberia and Milan.
- **€38–45k:** Paris, Amsterdam, Berlin, Munich, Vienna, Brussels and Luxembourg.
- **About CHF 76–89k:** Zurich.

Dublin is mixed: junior marketing executive pay is about €26k, but analyst and product roles pay €34–38k. Data for Porto, Copenhagen and Stockholm is thin. Their reference salaries below are labelled estimates.

### Inferences
- Strategy, consulting and graduate-programme roles in Madrid (Junior Analyst median ~€27.5k) pay slightly above junior marketing (~€22–26k). Big-4, strategy-consulting and FMCG graduate schemes were not retrieved city by city. They usually pay above these Glassdoor medians, so treat the reference salaries as a **mid-case, not a ceiling**.

### Gaps
- No reliable junior figure was found for Porto, Copenhagen or Stockholm (those references are estimates), or for "innovation analyst" titles in any city. Several Glassdoor snippets mislabel units (monthly shown as "per year").
- Levels.fyi covers marketing only at aggregate level. For example, Luxembourg's all-levels marketing range is €65.5k–93.2k — [Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/t/marketing/locations/luxembourg). That is not usable for juniors.

## Q2. Net monthly take-home (single, no children) at the reference salary

### Takeaway
Net/mo (annual net ÷ 12):
- Zurich ~€5,720, before health insurance
- Copenhagen ~€2,870 [est]
- Amsterdam €2,810
- Luxembourg €2,652
- Vienna €2,567
- Munich €2,436
- Dublin €2,434
- Stockholm ~€2,400
- Brussels ~€2,390 [est]
- Paris ~€2,350 [est]
- Berlin €2,301
- Milan €1,856
- Lisbon €1,854 with IRS Jovem in year 1, €1,588 without
- **Madrid €1,718**
- Barcelona €1,639
- Porto €1,632 with IRS Jovem in year 1, €1,425 without

### Cited Findings
**Madrid, €26,000** [calc]
- IRPF €3,735, Social Security €1,651, net €20,614/yr. That is €1,718/mo over 12, or €1,472 × 14 if paid in 14 instalments — [Talent.com calculator, Comunidad de Madrid 2026](https://es.talent.com/tax-calculator?salary=26000&from=year&region=Comunidad+de+Madrid)
- Not checked against the AEAT simulator.

**Barcelona, €25,000** [calc]
- IRPF €3,745, SS €1,588, net **€19,668/yr (€1,639/mo)** — [Talent.com, Cataluña 2026](https://es.talent.com/tax-calculator?salary=25000&from=year&region=Catalu%C3%B1a)

**Portugal, normal regime** [est]
- Inputs:
  - SS 11% — [CRN Contabilidade](https://crncontabilidade.pt/?p=20953)
  - 2026 IRS brackets: 12.5% to €8,342; 15.7% to €12,587; 21.2% to €17,838; 24.1% to €23,089; 31.1% to €29,397 — [Santander Salto](https://www.santander.pt/salto/escaloes-irs); confirmed by [CGD](https://www.cgd.pt/Site/Saldo-Positivo/leis-e-impostos/Pages/novidades-IRS.aspx) (rates in brackets 2–5 cut by 0.3 pp)
  - Specific deduction ≈ €4,587 (8.54 × IAS €537.13). The IAS value is from the earlier notes; the 8.54× multiplier is **not verified**.
  - Minus €250 general deduction.
- Results:
  - **Lisbon €25k → ~€19,057/yr (€1,588/mo)**
  - **Porto €22k → ~€17,098/yr (€1,425/mo)**
- Cross-checks:
  - NettoCalc gives €1,600/mo for €25k — [NettoCalc PT](https://nettocalc.com/pt/25000-euro)
  - A simulator example of €1,500 × 14 (€21k) gives about €1,113 net per month × 14 — [search summary of CRN/Coverflex simulators](https://www.coverflex.com/pt/blog/simulador-de-salario-liquido-da-coverflex-para-2026)

**Portugal, IRS Jovem year 1** [est]
- 100% of employment income is exempt up to €29,542.15, so only the 11% SS applies — [Employsome](https://employsome.com/hire/portugal/irs-jovem/)
- **Lisbon: €22,250/yr (€1,854/mo). Porto: €19,580/yr (€1,632/mo).**
- In years 2–4 (75% exempt), the researcher estimates roughly €1,820/mo in Lisbon.
- Payment is usually 14 instalments: for €25k, about €1,786 gross per instalment.

**Paris, €38,000** [calc + est]
- Net before income tax ≈ **€2,507/mo** (€30,084/yr). Net taxable income ≈ €2,624/mo — [Simulateur-Gratuit (URSSAF 2026 contributions)](https://simulateur-gratuit.fr/salaire-brut-net/annuel/38000-euros-brut-annuel/)

- **Model value: ~€2,354/mo [est].** The cadre route would be about €100 lower.

**Amsterdam, €40,000** [calc]
- Take-home **€33,714/yr (€2,810/mo)**. Box 1 tax and national insurance combined, no 30% ruling — [FiscalFold NL 2026](https://www.fiscalfold.com/tax/nl/40000/)
- The health premium is paid separately (see Q3).
- The zorgtoeslag income limit for a single person is €40,857 in 2026, so at €40k there is little or no allowance — [summary of Belastingdienst/Rijksoverheid figures](https://www.zorgwijzer.nl/zorgverzekering-2026/zorgtoeslag-omlaag-in-2026-bereken-hier-hoeveel-je-per-maand-krijgt)

### Inferences
- Madrid's net is low partly because its gross is low; its tax wedge (~21%) is moderate.
- IRS Jovem lifts Lisbon year-1 net **above Madrid** (+€136/mo) even though the gross salaries are similar.
- Italy and Belgium take the largest share of gross. Ireland and Luxembourg take relatively little at junior salary levels.

### Gaps
- Only Austria (karriere.at) and Ireland (income.ie and FinanceTool, which agree) were cross-checked with two calculators. ES, DE, LU and CH rely on Talent.com alone.
- France, Portugal, Belgium (annualisation) and Denmark are researcher estimates. **None was verified with the official simulators** (AEAT, Portal das Finanças, URSSAF/impots.gouv, Belastingdienst, BMF, SPF Finances, Skattestyrelsen).
- The Swedish municipal rate and the Danish allowances are not verified.

## Q3. Monthly costs: room vs 1-bed, transport, food and other living costs, utilities, mandatory health insurance

### Takeaway
Costs per month:
- **Rooms:** €475 (Porto) to €1,000 (Amsterdam). Estimated ~€1,100–1,220 in Dublin, Luxembourg and Zurich.
- **1-bed outside the centre:** €842 (Vienna) to €1,941 (Dublin and Zurich).
- **Youth transport pricing is a real lever:**
  - Madrid Abono Joven €10
  - Lisbon Navegante free up to age 23
  - Vienna Jahreskarte Jugend €300/yr (~€25/mo) under 26
  - Luxembourg free
  - Paris: employers must refund 50%
- **Mandatory health insurance on top of net pay:** only NL (~€157–159/mo) and CH (young adult avg CHF 326; ~CHF 385 at a Zurich insurer).
