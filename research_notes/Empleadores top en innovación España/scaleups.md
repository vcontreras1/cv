# Employee opinions on Spanish/European scaleups, unicorns and venture builders (innovation, strategy and growth roles)

**Method note (please read first):** Searches ran on 2026-10-12. Glassdoor, Indeed, Genbeta and similar sites **could not be fetched directly**. The tool returned DNS errors for glassdoor.es, uk.indeed.com and genbeta.com. That means every employee opinion below comes from **search-result snippets of the review pages, not the full page I would normally read**. Short phrases in "quotes" are fragments that appeared in those snippets. Treat them as close paraphrase or partial quotes. I could not check the full context, and for most of them I could not confirm the exact review date or the reviewer's role. Where the snippet gave a role or year, I include it. Ratings are Glassdoor/Indeed snapshots shown in search results, and they can vary by regional domain. No Reddit or Blind threads specific to Spain came up.

Sentiment tags: [+] positive, [-] negative, [~] mixed.

## 1. Career growth and learning speed: fast responsibility or chaos? Founders' associate, strategy & ops and growth roles for business grads

### Takeaway
The pattern is consistent across Glovo, Fever, Cabify, Factorial and Perk (ex-TravelPerk). People learn very fast and get early responsibility, sometimes from internship level. The same reviews describe unclear career paths, shifting priorities, high turnover and weak onboarding. In other words, growth often comes from the chaos itself, such as moving into a seat someone just left. Entry roles for business grads exist under titles like "Growth Strategy Analyst" and "Jr Operations & Strategy Analyst" (Glovo). I found no reliable Spain data for "Founders' Associate" roles.

### Cited Findings
- **Glovo [+]:** One reviewer says even an intern could work on "impactful projects". Another notes an option to "switch your team", which comes from the "high employee turnover rate". Undated, from the Glassdoor review listing snippet. — [Glassdoor Glovo reviews](https://static.glassdoor.com.ar/Reviews/Glovo-Reviews-E3424586_P3.htm)
- **Glovo [-]:** "not clear growth path, hard to grow". Another reviewer says promotion at senior levels favours a narrow profile (paraphrase). Undated. — [Glassdoor Glovo reviews](https://static.glassdoor.com.ar/Reviews/Glovo-Reviews-E3424586_P4.htm)
- **Glovo [~]:** An Account Manager review is titled "Collaborative team spirit but high expectations at times". The snippet says very ambitious targets can make work feel intense. Role: Account Manager, Glassdoor ES. — [Glassdoor review RVW104194433](https://static.glassdoor.es/Reviews/Employee-Review-Glovo-E3424586-RVW104194433.htm)
- **Factorial [+]:** A former SDR says they were promoted to Account Executive in seven months and then to Team Leader (paraphrase). — [Glassdoor ES, Team Leader reviews](https://www.glassdoor.es/Opiniones/Factorial-Team-Leader-Opiniones-EI_IE1618672.0,9_KO10,21.htm)
- **Factorial [~]:** Another reviewer says good work is rewarded and recognised regularly, but mentions high turnover and unclear retention policies (paraphrase). — [Glassdoor ES review](https://www.glassdoor.es/Opiniones/Opinión-empleado-Factorial-E1618672-RVW99044774.htm)
- **Fever [~]:** Reviewers praise how fast they learn, but describe a pace that "can be overwhelming" and say "onboardings have not been fully developed yet". Undated. — [Glassdoor Fever reviews](https://static.glassdoor.es/Reviews/Fever-Reviews-E768571_P26.htm)
- **Cabify [~]:** Reviewers mention an "accelerated learning curve" and friendly people. They also cite "burocracy", and one says some technically strong people were not promoted because they "were not friends with the right persons". Undated. — [Glassdoor Cabify reviews](https://static.glassdoor.es/Reviews/Cabify-Reviews-E975202_P30.htm)
- **Revolut [~]:** Reviewers praise the learning opportunities and the pay. Performance is judged "almost entirely by KPIs", targets are described as unrealistic, and several mention "hire and fire". Mostly 2024–2026 according to the search summary. — [Glassdoor IT review, Sales Rep](https://www.glassdoor.it/Recensioni/Recensione-dipendente-Revolut-E1176471-RVW94608403.htm); [Indeed reviews](https://uk.indeed.com/cmp/Revolut/locations/ENG/London)
- **Entry-level business roles at Glovo:** Postings exist for "Growth Strategy Analyst" (Barcelona, on-site), "Marketing Growth Analyst" and "Jr Operations & Strategy Analyst". — [Growth.Talent listing](https://www.growthtalent.org/jobs/growth-marketing/glovo/growth-strategy-analyst-glovo); [iAgora listing](https://www.iagora.com/work/de/jobs/bei-Glovo-27351). Delivery Hero (Glovo's parent) also runs an "Early Careers Booster" programme in Barcelona. — [Delivery Hero careers](https://careers.deliveryhero.com/job/early-careers-booster-in-barcelona-spain-jid-8623)
- **Founders' Associate roles:** One posting (DolarApp, not clearly Spain-based) asks for 2+ years at a VC-backed company, top-tier consulting or top-tier investment banking. That suggests these roles are not always open to fresh graduates. — [Hirebase listing](https://beta.hirebase.org/company/dolarapp/jobs/founders-associate)
- **Venture builders:** Antai (Wallapop, Chicplace) recruits experienced professionals to join new ventures as CEO or COO in exchange for equity. That model suits mid-career operators more than fresh graduates. — [Novobrief](https://novobrief.com/antai-startup-studio-investment-fund/); [Fundación Bankinter](https://www.fundacionbankinter.org/en/?p=29779)

### Inferences
- For a BBA graduate, the best entry points seem to be analyst-titled growth, strategy or ops roles at larger scaleups (Glovo/Delivery Hero, Cabify, Fever, Perk). Founders' Associate roles seem to want prior experience, typically consulting or banking.
- Fast learning and chaos are two sides of the same thing in these reviews. Turnover opens doors, but onboarding and mentoring are weak.

### Gaps
- No verifiable employee reviews of working at venture builders (Antai, Lanzadera, Wayra, Bcombinator, SeedRocket).
- No reviews specific to Founders' Associate or Strategy & Ops roles at Spanish scaleups.
- I could not verify exact review dates or many reviewer roles because the review pages could not be opened.

## 2. Work environment, culture, burnout, layoffs (2023–2026) and leadership quality

### Takeaway
Glassdoor ratings for Spanish scaleups mostly fall between about 3.4 and 4.0. Glovo (3.4) and Fever (3.5) are below their industry average, and Perk (about 4.0) and Cabify (3.9) are at or above it. Burnout and constant shifts in direction are recurring complaints. There were layoffs or labour disputes at Glovo (2023, 2024, 2026), Wallbox (repeated cuts) and Holaluz (2025). Revolut is the European case people point to for a high-pressure, KPI-driven culture.

### Cited Findings
**Ratings (Glassdoor/Indeed snapshots shown in search results, 2025–2026):**
- **Glovo:** 3.4/5 from 1,380 reviews; 57% would recommend; IT industry average 3.9. — [Glassdoor Glovo](https://static.glassdoor.com.ar/Reviews/Glovo-Reviews-E3424586_P3.htm)
- **Perk (TravelPerk):** about 4.0/5 from about 390 reviews; 81–84% recommend; 86–88% CEO approval (varies by snapshot). — [Glassdoor Perk](https://static.glassdoor.es/Reviews/Perk-Reviews-E1174739_P10.htm)
- **Fever:** 3.5/5 from 352 reviews. — [Glassdoor Fever](https://static.glassdoor.es/Reviews/Fever-Reviews-E768571_P111.htm)
- **Cabify:** 3.9/5 from 105 reviews; 79–82% recommend. — [Glassdoor Cabify](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Cabify-Reviews-E975202.htm)
- **Factorial:** 3.7/5 among Account Executives (45 reviews) and 3.6 among Senior SWE (6 reviews). I found no overall figure. — [Glassdoor ES AE](https://www.glassdoor.es/Opiniones/Factorial-Account-Executive-Opiniones-EI_IE1618672.0,9_KO10,27.htm)
- **Revolut:** Indeed 3.4/5 overall (culture 3.7, management 3.6). — [Indeed Revolut](https://in.indeed.com/cmp/Revolut/reviews)
- **N26:** Glassdoor 3.5/5 (702 reviews); Blind 2.9/5 (47 reviews), with management rated lowest. — [Glassdoor N26](https://static-pc.glassdoor.de/Reviews/N26-Reviews-E1032903_P49.htm)
- **Holaluz:** Indeed ES 2.8/5 (15 reviews); stability/promotion and management sub-scores both 2.4. — [Indeed Holaluz](https://es.indeed.com/cmp/Holaluz/reviews)

**Opinions:**
- **Glovo [-]:** One review describes a toxic atmosphere and constant burnout, including colleagues "crying in the bathroom" (paraphrase plus fragment, single strongly worded review, undated). Other reviews cite unclear priorities and an "often changing policy of the company". — [Glassdoor Glovo](https://static.glassdoor.com.ar/Reviews/Glovo-Reviews-E3424586_P4.htm)
- **Factorial [-]:** "the turnover rate is alarming". A separate reviewer says people "get pushed out for speaking up or disagreeing" with leadership. An Account Executive reports pay well below market and constantly changing commissions (paraphrase). — [Glassdoor ES review](https://www.glassdoor.es/Opiniones/Opinión-empleado-Factorial-E1618672-RVW100610660.htm); [Glassdoor ES AE](https://www.glassdoor.es/Opiniones/Factorial-Account-Executive-Opiniones-EI_IE1618672.0,9_KO10,27.htm)
- **Factorial, review integrity concern [-]:** A 2025 review alleges the company posted "Over 200 positive reviews" within a few weeks after its rating fell. Factorial replied by inviting current employees to share feedback with People. — [Glassdoor ES review](https://www.glassdoor.es/Opiniones/Opinión-empleado-Factorial-E1618672-RVW102272495.htm). *Note: this weakens how far Factorial's aggregate rating can be trusted.*
- **Perk (TravelPerk) [+], 2025 reviews:** Company events make people feel valued, the team is multinational and cross-cultural, "everyone gets equity in the company", and there is an allowance for AI tools (paraphrase). — [Glassdoor Perk](https://static.glassdoor.es/Reviews/Perk-Reviews-E1174739_P9.htm)
- **Perk [-], 2025 reviews:** In SDR roles, "metrics are very important, therefore, very stressful". Other complaints: a weak share scheme, tech debt, limited remote work. One 2023 review calls the environment "toxic" (single review). — [Glassdoor Perk](https://static.glassdoor.es/Reviews/Perk-Reviews-E1174739_P20.htm)
- **Revolut [-]:** A Glassdoor ES review by an Operations Manager is titled "Toxic culture". Others are titled "Toxic and Unsupportive Workplace" (Sales Representative) and "Toxic environment" (Product Designer). Blind reviewers describe unpleasant office politics in London. — [Glassdoor ES Ops Manager](https://static.glassdoor.es/Reviews/Employee-Review-Revolut-E1176471-RVW92247549.htm); [Glassdoor IT Product Designer](https://www.glassdoor.it/Recensioni/Recensione-dipendente-Revolut-E1176471-RVW84752368.htm); [Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/company/Revolut/reviews?page=1)
- **Holaluz [-]:** An Indeed reviewer writes "te dicen que es flexible, pero no lo es" ("they tell you it's flexible, but it isn't"). — [Indeed Holaluz](https://es.indeed.com/cmp/Holaluz/reviews)
- **Celonis [~]:** A Glassdoor review cites "lots of layoffs". The company replied that it is going through "a time of change". In 2023 its CPO said turnover was "on the mid to lower end of the benchmark". — [Glassdoor IT Celonis](https://www.glassdoor.it/Recensioni/Celonis-Recensioni-E1307503.htm); [Unleash, Mar 2023](https://unleash.ai/talent-management/interview/how-celonis-has-avoided-layoffs)

**Layoffs and labour conflict, 2023–2026:**
- **Glovo:** 250 jobs cut in 2023 (6.5% of global staff, mostly Barcelona). — [Xataka](https://www.xataka.com/empresas-y-economia/startups-espanolas-tampoco-se-libran-despidos-glovo-recortara-6-5-su-plantilla); [Alimarket](https://www.alimarket.es/alimentacion/noticia_amp/365235/glovo-despedira-al-6-5--de-su-plantilla-global--250-trabajadores-principalmente-con-sede-en-barcelona). Reported further cuts: 25 in Barcelona (April 2024) and 22 in Madrid (May 2024). In March 2026 it announced an ERE for up to 750 couriers (couriers, not corporate staff). — [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/economia/glovo-anuncia-ere-750-repartidores-medio-denuncias-multiples-despidos-individuales_1_13059750.amp.html)
- **Wallbox:** Proposed 156 dismissals in Spain. A later report says it has cut about a third of its workforce and is restructuring its debt. — [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/catalunya/firma-cargadores-coches-electricos-wallbox-plantea-despedir-156-trabajadores-espana_1_9930634.html); [Xataka](https://www.xataka.com/movilidad/wallbox-entra-barrena-vale-que-su-deuda-nadie-sabe-que-hacer-ella)
- **Holaluz:** In early 2025 it unilaterally changed working conditions, removing telework and other benefits. Unions called a strike, and about 25% of staff reportedly left rather than accept the new terms (another outlet said 35%, which I could not verify). In June 2025 the company agreed to negotiate to avoid going to court. — [Genbeta](https://www.genbeta.com/actualidad/25-empleados-holaluz-dejan-su-trabajo-cinco-anos-teletrabajo-empresa-obligan-a-volver-a-oficina); [Merca2, Jun 2025](https://www.merca2.es/2025/06/03/holaluz-negociar-evitar-juicio-2197819/)
- **N26:** Older reporting (2020–21) describes about 300 departures and low trust in management. I found nothing on 2024–25. — [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/job-cuts-new-hires/n26-loses-300-employees-and-head-of-hr-in-12-months)

### Inferences
- Among the Spanish scaleups with data, Perk and Cabify look like the most stable cultures. Glovo, Fever and Factorial show more burnout and turnover signals. Revolut is a high-pay, high-pressure option.
- Being climate-focused did not protect Wallbox or Holaluz from layoffs or labour conflict. A strong mission does not guarantee stability.

### Gaps
- No recent ratings or reviews found for Seedtag, Jobandtalent, Typeform, Wallapop, Idealista, Clarity AI, Multiverse Computing, Personio, Klarna, Spotify Spain or Mistral.
- I found no reports of TravelPerk/Perk layoffs in 2024–25 (none appeared in these searches, which does not prove there were none).
- I could not confirm whether Factorial's 2025 surge of positive reviews was organic.

## 3. Sense of purpose and impact, and junior salaries and equity in Spain

### Takeaway
I found almost no independent employee testimony about purpose. Companies whose mission is explicitly about impact (Clarity AI on sustainability data, Holaluz on 100% green energy, Wallbox on EV charging) state their purpose clearly. However, the only independent signals for Holaluz and Wallbox are negative ones about stability. Junior business-analyst pay in Madrid and Barcelona is about €28–38k gross a year. Scaleups sometimes add equity: Perk employees say everyone gets equity, though some call the scheme weak.

### Cited Findings
- **Clarity AI (Madrid):** Its stated mission is "bringing societal impact to markets" through sustainability tech. Values: fact-based, diverse, meritocratic, transparent, flexible. Its culture page quotes an associate director saying the "mission and vision were appealing to me", but this testimonial is curated by the company. More than 300 employees. — [Clarity AI culture page](https://clarity.ai/our-mission); [Greenhouse job post](https://job-boards.eu.greenhouse.io/clarityai/jobs/4653187101). It also hires interns, e.g. "Product Research & Innovation Intern (Regulation)". — [Built In](https://builtin.com/job/product-research-innovation-intern-regulation/7869814)
- **Holaluz:** Describes itself as an energy-transition tech company aiming for a world run 100% on green energy. Employee sentiment from 2025 is poor (see section 2). — [Indeed Holaluz](https://es.indeed.com/cmp/Holaluz)
- **Glovo [+], purpose/impact:** An intern said they could work on "impactful projects". This is impact in the sense of business influence, not social impact. — [Glassdoor Glovo](https://static.glassdoor.com.ar/Reviews/Glovo-Reviews-E3424586_P3.htm)
- **Junior salaries in Spain:**
  - Business Analyst, junior: Madrid €28–36k, Barcelona €28–38k gross a year (2026, JobRise blog estimate). — [JobRise Madrid](https://jobrise.io/es/blog/salario-business-analyst-madrid-2026/); [JobRise Barcelona](https://jobrise.io/es/blog/salario-business-analyst-barcelona-2026/)
  - Spain overall, 0–2 years experience: €24–32k (blog estimate). The same sources say an experienced fintech analyst in Barcelona (3 years) can reach €45–50k. — [JobRise Spain](https://jobrise.io/es/blog/cuanto-gana-business-analyst-espana-2026/)
  - Glovo Growth Strategy Analyst (Barcelona): third-party estimate of $45–65k based on 9 similar roles. This is not reported pay and is given in USD. — [Growth.Talent](https://www.growthtalent.org/jobs/growth-marketing/glovo/growth-strategy-analyst-glovo)
  - Spanish VC associates, for comparison: one Madrid associate earned €36k plus an €8k bonus, while others reached up to €75k. That points to a large gender and firm-level pay gap. — [El Referente](https://elreferente.es/?p=174352)
  - Germany Founders' Associate, as a reference only: about €35k typical (€20.5–42k). — [Jooble DE](https://de.jooble.org/salary/founder%27s-associate)
- **Equity:** Perk reviewers say "everyone gets equity in the company", while others call the share scheme weak (2025). — [Glassdoor Perk](https://static.glassdoor.es/Reviews/Perk-Reviews-E1174739_P10.htm). Factorial AE reviewers report below-market pay and changing commissions. — [Glassdoor ES AE](https://www.glassdoor.es/Opiniones/Factorial-Account-Executive-Opiniones-EI_IE1618672.0,9_KO10,27.htm)
- **Talent attraction:** Glovo uses English as its working language and offers relocation and visa sponsorship for high-skill tech roles (2025 list from a business school). — [ESEI](https://www.eseibusinessschool.com/barcelonas-top-startups-hiring-interna/)

### Inferences
- For a graduate who values purpose, Clarity AI (Madrid, sustainability data) best matches "impact plus innovation plus strategy" on paper. Independent employee opinions on it are missing and should be checked directly.
- A realistic junior package at Spanish scaleups for business roles is about €28–38k base, with equity sometimes on top. Revolut and the larger unicorns probably pay above this, but I found no verified Spain-specific junior figures.

### Gaps
- No Glassdoor ratings or independent reviews found for Clarity AI, Multiverse Computing, Ashoka Spain, or Spanish B Corps' innovation teams.
- No verified Holaluz B Corp status found in this search, so it should be checked.
- No company-specific junior salary data, from Glassdoor or offers, for Glovo, Cabify, Factorial, Perk or Revolut Spain. No equity amounts.
- I did not find the LinkedIn Top Startups Spain list or Sifted/El Referente coverage of employee sentiment.
