# Top innovation employers hiring junior (0–2 yrs, full-time) business profiles in South-West Europe: Madrid (baseline), Barcelona, Lisbon, Porto, Paris, Dublin, Milan

Research date: 2026-10-26. Built for Valeria (BBA Comillas ICADE, June 2026; EU national; Spanish native, English C1; digital marketing/strategy; values purpose; wants to save money).

**How to read the salary data**
- Glassdoor "company in city" pages show ranges as "€X–€Y /yr, N salaries submitted". These are the most reliable figures here, and I quote them with N. Pages were scraped through Firecrawl on 2026-10-26; Glassdoor shows no update date beyond "latest salary added X days ago".
- Glassdoor's headline "estimated average ... $" figures (for example "Glovo Business Analyst €108,269" or "Revolut Lisbon $108,806–$192,744") are clearly inflated against the submitted EUR ranges on the same site. **Do not use them.** They are flagged where they appear.
- Glassdoor ratings shown next to a salary or role page can be global or role-level, not city-level. The scope is labelled each time.
- Careers links marked "(standard URL, not opened)" are the companies' usual careers domains and were not checked on 2026-10-26.
- Madrid data mostly comes from earlier notes in `Empleadores top en innovación España/` and `Innovación en Europa para recién graduada/`. Their original source URLs are repeated here, and I did not re-check them. To avoid duplication, see `ofertas_junior.md` for live postings and `seguros_santalucia.md` for insurtech.

## 1. Madrid (baseline): who to target, pay, rating

### Takeaway
Madrid is the baseline. Big Spanish corporates' graduate programmes pay about €30–35K fixed (BBVA, Repsol). Consulting and big tech pay more: Accenture consultant about €38K base, Amazon vendor manager €48–72K with 1–6 years. MBB pays the most but is the most selective. Iberdrola, Repsol and BBVA score well on stability and benefits, and badly on promotion speed.

## 2. Barcelona

### Takeaway
Glovo is the clearest junior business employer in Barcelona. It has a large salary sample: Business Analyst €32–38K from 55 salaries. That is roughly in line with Madrid, not above it. Amazon Barcelona cut 791 corporate jobs (Dec 2025 agreement) and Glovo ran a rider ERE in 2026. Factorial's sales pay is low and its sales-team rating is weak. King's Barcelona rating is poor (2.8).

### Cited Findings
**Glovo (Delivery Hero), quick-commerce and delivery marketplace, HQ in Barcelona**
- Glassdoor Glovo Barcelona (2,749 salaries, scraped 2026-10-26): **Business Analyst €32K–38K/yr (55 salaries)**, Data Analyst €34–44K (55), Project Manager €40–55K (51), Product Manager €60–80K (60). — [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Glovo-Barcelona-Salaries-EI_IE3424586.0,5_IL.6,15_IC2547194.htm)
- Levels.fyi: Glovo Business Analyst in Greater Barcelona ranges from about $45.6K (L2) to $38.6K (L3), as shown in the snippet. The level ordering looks inconsistent. — [Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/glovo/salaries/business-analyst/locations/greater-barcelona-area)
- Glassdoor's "estimated average €108,269" for Glovo Business Analyst in Spain (rating 4.1 from 8 reviews) is **not credible** next to the 55 submitted salaries above. — [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Glovo-Business-Analyst-Spain-Salaries-EJI_IE3424586.0,5_KO6,22_IL.23,28_IN219.htm)
- Junior role types: Business Analyst (for example "Business Analyst Marketplace Experience" appeared in Glassdoor job listings), Jr Account Manager, and the Early Careers Booster rotations (Marketing & Growth, Ops, Finance). See `ofertas_junior.md`. — [Glassdoor jobs](https://www.glassdoor.com/Jobs/Glovo-Product-Manager-Barcelona-Spain-Jobs-EI_IE3424586.0,5_KO6,21_IL.22,37_IM1015.htm)
- Language: native or fluent Spanish plus English for Spain-market roles (from the Jr Logistics AM posting in `ofertas_junior.md`).
- Layoffs: Glovo announced an ERE for about 750 riders on 11 Mar 2026. The 13 May 2026 agreement cut it to 436, with 37 days' severance per year. This affects riders, not office staff. — [El País](https://elpais.com/economia/2026-03-11/glovo-anuncia-un-ere-que-afectara-a-unos-750-repartidores.html); [El Periódico](https://www.elperiodico.com/es/economia/20260513/glovo-ere-acuerdo-despidos-repartidores-130201774)
- Careers: careers.deliveryhero.com (Glovo jobs are listed there).

**Amazon (Barcelona corporate offices)**
- An ERE was agreed on 17 Dec 2025: **791 dismissals in Barcelona corporate offices**, down from 978. Terms were 38 days/year (max 24 months, minimum €7,000), with voluntary exits and more than 100 people relocated within the group. It was part of about 14,000 global corporate cuts. — [ARA](https://es.ara.cat/economia/mercado-laboral/acuerdo-ere-amazon-reducen-791-despidos-barcelona_1_5595037.html); [Forbes ES](https://forbes.es/economia/847519/acuerdo-para-reducir-los-despidos-del-ere-de-amazon-en-barcelona-hasta-791/); [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.es/noticias/empresas/amazon-cierra-sus-ere-en-espana-con-el-despido-de-920-trabajadores/2025121847909)

**Factorial (HR software scale-up)**
- RepVue, which is sales-focused, rates Factorial **2.8 (62 ratings)**. Spain base pay (updated Dec 2025, 18 salaries) runs from about €18.6K for an SDR to about €28.0K for a Mid-Market AE, before variable pay. Sources disagree on quota attainment (23% vs 39.7%). — [RepVue Spain](https://www.repvue.com/companies/FactorialHR/salaries/countries/ES); [RepVue](https://repvue.com/companies/FactorialHR/salaries)
- A "Founding Account Executive" role in Barcelona was listed at €30–70K OTE, posted 30 Jul 2026. — [Point Nine jobs](https://jobs.pointnine.com/companies/factorial-hr-2/jobs/88101557-founding-account-executive)

**Wallbox (EV charging)**
- The last confirmed restructuring was in 2023: 92 dismissals in Barcelona after it initially proposed about 156, plus the closure of a Sant Andreu de la Barca plant. I found no 2025–2026 ERE. — [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/catalunya/acuerdo-despedir-92-trabajadores-firma-cargadores-coches-electricos-wallbox_1_9983109.html); [Híbridos y Eléctricos](https://www.hibridosyelectricos.com/coches/esta-marca-espanola-cargadores-coches-electricos-ha-cerrado-sus-fabricas-barcelona_73114_102.html)

**King (gaming), from earlier notes**
- King Barcelona has 2.8/5 from 20 reviews, with career development at 2.2. One review is titled *"King: Un sueño convertido en una pesadilla"*. — [Glassdoor King Barcelona](https://www.glassdoor.es/Opiniones/King-Barcelona-Opiniones-EI_IE597128.0,4_IL.5,14_IC2547194.htm)

- Market benchmark: Business Analyst in Barcelona averages €36,000 (Glassdoor 2026). — [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/barcelona-spain-business-analyst-salary-SRCH_IL.0,15_IM1015_KO16,32.htm)

### Inferences
- In Barcelona a junior business salary is about €32–38K, the same as Madrid, and rent is similar. It offers no savings advantage over Madrid. The draw is the scale-up ecosystem (Glovo, Factorial, Perk), not the pay.
- Factorial's low base pay and weak RepVue score make it a poor fit for someone who wants to save.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve company-specific junior pay or ratings for Perk (TravelPerk), HP Barcelona or the Revolut Barcelona hub.
- I found no Glassdoor company rating for Factorial; only RepVue.

## 3. Lisbon

### Takeaway
Lisbon fintech and AI hubs pay junior business and ops staff much less than Madrid. Revolut FinCrime Analyst is about €1–2K/month (15 salaries) and Account Executive about €2–4K/month (6). Feedzai's submitted salaries are low even for tech. The Lisbon roles open to a Spanish speaker are mostly ops, fincrime or sales. Revolut's AE band is the best junior business pay found in Lisbon.

### Cited Findings
**Revolut (fintech, large Lisbon ops hub)**
- Glassdoor Revolut Lisbon (166 salaries, scraped 2026-10-26): **FinCrime Analyst €1–2K/month (15)**, **Account Executive €2–4K/month (6)**, Customer Support Specialist €1–2K/month (6), Data Analyst €3–5K/month (6), Operations Manager €3–7K/month (5). — [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Revolut-Lisbon-Salaries-EI_IE1176471.0,7_IL.8,14_IM1121.htm)
- Glassdoor FinCrime Analyst Lisbon P25–P75 of $49K–$90K (4 salaries) contradicts the €1–2K/month above. The USD estimate is **unreliable**. — [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Revolut-Fincrime-Analyst-Lisbon-Portugal-Salaries-EJI_IE1176471.0,7_KO8,24_IL.25,40_IM1121.htm)
- Rating: the Lisbon salaries page displays "4.3 (6,413)". That is probably a global widget, and it conflicts with Revolut's widely reported lower culture scores. **Treat it as unverified.** — [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Revolut-Lisbon-Salaries-EI_IE1176471.0,7_IL.8,14_IM1121.htm)
- Careers: revolut.com/careers (standard URL, not opened).

**Feedzai (AI fraud prevention, Portuguese unicorn)**
- Glassdoor Feedzai Lisbon (286 salaries): Product Manager €42–55K (10), Project Manager €43–66K (5), Software Engineer €26–38K (55), Data Scientist €28–36K (35), Intern €9–12K (7). I found no junior business or marketing title. — [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Feedzai-Lisbon-Salaries-EI_IE744720.0,7_IL.8,14_IC3192045.htm)
- Rating 3.7/5 (323), shown on the salaries page. — [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Feedzai-Lisbon-Salaries-EI_IE744720.0,7_IL.8,14_IC3192045.htm)
- Opinion (verbatim, date not visible): *"Compensation is awful. The company builds an average salary based on low-demanding companies which (obviously) pay low salaries. Also, no equity for employees."* — [Glassdoor SG compensation reviews](https://www.glassdoor.sg/Reviews/Feedzai-compensation-Reviews-EI_IE744720.0,7_KH8,20.htm)
- Careers: feedzai.com/careers (standard URL, not opened).

**OutSystems, Unbabel, Remote, Mercedes-Benz.io**: no company-specific junior business pay was retrieved. I found no 2025–2026 layoff reports for Feedzai, OutSystems or Unbabel; Unbabel's last reported cut was about 35% in April 2020. — [Observador](https://observador.pt/2020/04/08/unbabel-vai-despedir-35-dos-funcionarios-sao-mais-de-80-pessoas/)

**Farfetch status**: in Feb 2024 there were reports it could cut about 1,000 people in Portugal after the Coupang takeover (based on employee forum messages), and a headline said 25–30% of its global workforce would go. I could not confirm the 2025–2026 status. — [ECO](https://eco.sapo.pt/2024/02/16/farfetch-podera-despedir-1-000-pessoas-em-portug...); [Notícias ao Minuto](https://www.noticiasaominuto.com/economia/2503091/farfetch-vai-reduzir-entre-25-a-30-dos-trabalhadores-a-nivel-global)

**Roland Berger Lisbon Junior BA**: it requires fluent Portuguese and may be internship-like. See `ofertas_junior.md`.

### Inferences
- Lisbon junior ops and fincrime pay of about €12–24K/year is well below Madrid's €30K+. Only sales (Revolut AE, €24–48K/year from a monthly band of 6 samples) or PM-track roles approach Madrid levels. Lower Lisbon rents do not make up the gap, so Lisbon is poor for saving at junior level.
- English-only roles exist at the international hubs (Revolut, Feedzai). Portuguese is needed for local consulting.

### Gaps
- I found no Glassdoor figures for Sword Health Lisbon, OutSystems, Remote or Mercedes-Benz.io business roles. I verified no 2025–2026 Revolut layoffs, and no Revolut Lisbon-specific culture quote.

## 4. Porto

### Takeaway
Porto has engineering-led employers (Sword Health, Critical Software, bank tech hubs) with few junior business roles and low pay. Sword Health has weak ratings: 2.7 globally and 3.3 in Porto. Porto is not a good savings or business-career fit for Valeria.

### Cited Findings
**Sword Health (digital physiotherapy and AI health; purpose-driven)**
- Glassdoor Sword Porto (138 salaries): Product Owner €29–30K (3), Product Manager €24–63K (3), Software Engineer €30–35K (11). These are mostly tech roles with tiny business samples. — [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Sword-Health-Porto-Salaries-EI_IE3848158.0,12_IL.13,18_IC3183562.htm)
- Ratings: global 2.7/5 (279), described as "21% below average for Healthcare employers". Porto 3.3/5 (41). Compensation 2.7, down 8% in 12 months. "SWORD Health (Portugal)" page: 2.9 (6), 33% recommend. — [Glassdoor overview](https://www.glassdoor.com/Overview/Working-at-Sword-Health-EI_IE3848158.11,23.htm); [Glassdoor Porto reviews](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Sword-Health-Porto-Oporto-Reviews-EI_IE3848158.0,12_IL.13,25_IC3183562.htm); [Glassdoor salary reviews](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Sword-Health-salary-Reviews-EI_IE3848158.0,12_KH13,19.htm); [Glassdoor PT](https://www.glassdoor.com/Overview/Working-at-SWORD-Health-Portugal-EI_IE7760582.11,32.htm)
- Careers: swordhealth.com/careers (standard URL, not opened).

**Critical Software (safety-critical software; aerospace, energy, health)**
- Rating about 3.5/5 (333 reviews) on one Glassdoor domain and 3.3/5 (336) on another. Reviewers repeatedly call pay below average. A Porto business analyst reviewer said it pays less than other big companies. Positives are benefits, learning and "a good place to start a career". Pay & benefits 3.0 on Indeed. ### Inferences
- If Valeria wants Portugal, Lisbon's international hubs beat Porto for business roles.

### Gaps
- I retrieved no data on Natixis or BNP tech hubs in Porto, and no Critical Software junior business salary figure.

Dublin pays by far the most for junior business roles. Google Associate Account Strategist is €54–75K (140 salaries) and Account Strategist €72–110K (302). Stripe Dublin AEs report €105–176K (43), but those are not entry-level. Expect sales and ops-flavoured roles. 2026 brought layoffs at TikTok (up to 667 roles at risk), Google (240 planned), Twilio and Microsoft.
